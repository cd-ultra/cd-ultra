WebCut

Video editor

A no-install, non-linear video editor that runs entirely in the browser. Your project files and rendered output never leave your computer — decoding, GPU compositing, and green-screen keying all happen locally. Multi-track timeline with frame-accurate scrubbing, razor split and snapping, plus a WebGPU chroma-key with optional neural matting.

  • WebGPU
  • WebCodecs
  • onnxruntime-web

BlobTune

Audio / pitch editor

An educational, from-scratch take on Melodyne. Load audio (or sing into your mic), and a hand-written YIN pitch tracker detects each note and draws it as a draggable "blob" on a piano roll. Drag to retune, time-stretch, preserve formants, view a live spectrogram, and import/export MIDI and WAV — all in plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step and no libraries.

  • JavaScript
  • Web Audio API
  • Canvas
  • DSP · YIN · TD-PSOLA
  • Zero-dependency
